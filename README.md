# 💄 NYKAA Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Nykaa Sales Analytics Dashboard** created in Power BI to analyze revenue, customers, products, brands, categories, campaigns, payment methods, cities, and sales channels.

The dashboard is designed as an executive-level business intelligence report that converts beauty e-commerce data into clear and actionable insights.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Nykaa Sales Analytics Dashboard](https://github.com/Neha3020/NYKAA-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard-PowerBI/blob/main/nykaa-sales-dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build an interactive sales analytics solution for a beauty and cosmetics business.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor:

- Revenue performance
- Customer base
- Order volume
- Customer ratings and satisfaction
- Average order value
- Monthly revenue trends
- Category performance
- Brand performance
- City-wise revenue
- Payment method contribution
- Campaign performance
- Sales channel performance
- Top-performing products and categories

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend
Tracks total revenue month-by-month to identify growth patterns, fluctuations, and high-performing periods.

### 2. Revenue by Category
Analyzes revenue contribution across major beauty categories such as:

- Men's Grooming
- Wellness
- Bath & Body
- Fragrance
- Hair Care
- Beauty Tools
- Makeup
- Skin Care

### 3. Revenue by City
Provides a city-level comparison of revenue to identify the strongest geographic markets.

### 4. Revenue by Brand
Ranks brands based on revenue contribution and helps identify leading brands within the portfolio.

### 5. Revenue by Payment Method
Analyzes revenue generated through different payment methods, helping understand customer payment preferences.

### 6. Revenue by Campaign
Compares campaign performance across:

- Regular
- Winter
- Festive
- Summer Sale

### 7. Customer & Product Insights
The dashboard highlights important business indicators including:

- Top Product
- Best Sales Channel
- Top Brand
- Best City
- Best-Selling Category
